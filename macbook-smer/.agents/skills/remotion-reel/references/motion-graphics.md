# Editorial treatment and motion graphics

Use the project's `brand/BRAND.md` for density, captions, colours and references. This is the story-to-image method from the supplied skill, adapted to Adam's direction.

## Read the whole story

Identify hook, setup/context, development, turn and payoff. Preserve the selected speech's meaning. For Fiki, plan and implement every sentence under [edit každej vety](../../../../reference/xvadur_klipy/brand/BRAND.md#fiki--edit-každej-vety), then verify its treatment in the render. Choose scene duration and continuity from the content. Source expression or delivery can carry a moment through a specifically timed framing, hold or return assigned to that sentence.

## Give the picture a job

| Job | Material and behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Prove | The real source, figure, screenshot or footage; show the relevant detail legibly. |
| Explain | A concrete analogy, comparison or diagram whose state changes with the explanation. |
| Show | The named thing, action, person or place; verify a specific referent. |
| Feel / joke | A reaction, meme or custom illustration with a setup, action and payoff. |
| Emphasize | Framing, movement, typography or sound that lands on the important speech beat. |

For each use decide the actual picture/action, composition, source trim, speech anchor, sound onset and exit. A literal keyword match is only a candidate. Compare what happens in the usable interval with the intended situation.

## Choose or create the material

Search, image generation, local reuse and a purpose-built scene are available according to the idea. A made-to-measure image may be layered, moved, revealed or combined with real footage. A generated illustration cannot prove an actual event or statistic. Use the existing source frame when it supplies the strongest contextual image.

Fiki favors contextual B-roll, GIFs and memes with rich combinations. The Smer direction is more reserved and driven by a strong argument. Neither requires a prebuilt asset collection. A scene-specific object is not automatically a reusable default for later clips.

## Compose and time

Choose a layout for the section: source full frame, full-screen insert over the original voice, split, PiP, document/photo treatment or custom composition. Keep the selected caption component readable. Overlay size and placement come from the visual's job and the real frame, not a universal requirement to cover the face.

Map the setup, reveal, hit and return to real source word/cut anchors. A graphic may enter before the key word when anticipation is intentional; land its payoff at the intended beat. Layout changes and zooms can follow semantic beats as well as existing cuts. Show enough of the actual action for the joke to read.

Use a coherent visual language from the brand sheet. A different topic does not require replacing the channel's style family. Color, texture, shape, collage and generated imagery are choices governed by this project's brief; the donor creator's personal bans are not imported.

## Typography and sound

Speech captions keep the project treatment. Large designed type can emphasize a line; avoid competing duplicate text and preserve intelligibility if a dedicated type scene temporarily replaces the speech-caption layer. Do not silence or move the spoken words to fit a decorative element.

Sound is part of the action: measure the lead-in to the actual SFX hit, mix against the voice, and check the result. A repeated pop on every caption word is not an automatic sound design.

## Inspect the result

Inspect setup, peak and exit frames in the actual render. Confirm the media plays the intended action, the graphic communicates its job, the relevant text fits, the face/captions remain intentional, and timing matches the words. Compare with the source reference mechanism and earlier revision. Sampled stills establish sampled evidence; motion and listening claims need their actual review.

When Adam asks for an edit, make these choices and execute. When he explicitly asks for concepts first, propose concrete options and wait for that requested choice. Per-beat playable review is described in `storyboard.md`.
